## The Post-Purchase Panic at Checkout

Three minutes after hitting "Place Order," a buyer realizes they picked apartment 2B instead of 2C. Or they selected the 2-pound bag of organic spelt flour instead of the 25-pound bulk sack.

In traditional e-commerce, this instant triggers panic. The customer sends an email marked "URGENT," fires off a chat message, and opens a contact ticket. Meanwhile, in the warehouse, the order prints, gets picked, and ships before a support representative even logs into helpdesk software on Monday morning.

The result is double-shipping costs, expensive return labels, bad customer reviews, and a customer support team drowning in routine post-purchase tickets.

For high-volume merchants managing thousands of SKUs, post-purchase order management is one of the largest hidden drains on margin. Giving buyers the power to **edit orders after purchase on Shopify** within a controlled timeframe changes the entire dynamic.

## What is the ShopGuide Action Window?

The **Action Window** is a configurable time limit in the ShopGuide dashboard—set in minutes by the merchant—during which our AI agent is authorized to process order edits autonomously.

Instead of waiting for a support agent to manually open Shopify Admin and edit line items, the customer interacts directly with the on-page ShopGuide agent. The agent verifies the order ID and customer email, accepts the requested edit, checks stock availability via the live Shopify Catalog API, and updates the order status immediately.

## How to Configure the Action Window in ShopGuide

Setting up post-purchase order editing in your ShopGuide dashboard takes less than two minutes:

1. **Navigate to Settings:** In your Shopify Admin, open **ShopGuide** and select **Settings**.
2. **Define Your Action Window Duration:** Set your desired window in minutes (e.g., 15, 30, or 60 minutes) based on how quickly your warehouse processes orders.
3. **Select Authorized Actions:** Enable address updates, variant exchanges, or order holds.
4. **Save & Test:** Use the **Test Your Guide** tool in the admin sidebar to simulate an order edit conversation.

By giving buyers self-serve control during those critical post-purchase minutes, you turn immediate regret into delight while protecting your support team from burnout.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does a customer edit an order after purchase on Shopify using ShopGuide?**
After checkout, the customer opens the ShopGuide on-page agent on your store. They provide their order number and email. The agent verifies their identity and, if the request is within the merchant's configured **Action Window**, allows them to update shipping addresses, swap product variants, or adjust item quantities automatically.

**What is the 'Action Window' in ShopGuide?**
The Action Window is a merchant-defined timeframe (set in minutes in your ShopGuide dashboard) during which the AI agent is authorized to perform autonomous order modifications. Once the Action Window expires and fulfillment processing starts, the agent directs complex requests to your human support team via Slack or your helpdesk.

**Will automated order edits cause inventory or payment mismatches?**
No. ShopGuide interacts directly with the native **Shopify Catalog API**. When a variant swap occurs, the agent checks live inventory levels first. If the new item has a different price, ShopGuide triggers standard Shopify checkout adjustments so your financial reporting and inventory logs remain clean.

**Can I control which order changes customers are allowed to make?**
Yes. In the ShopGuide settings, you can toggle specific permissions—such as allowing address changes only, permitting variant swaps, or authorizing full order cancellations—to align with your store's fulfillment policies.

**How does ShopGuide reduce support tickets for high-SKU stores?**
Up to 70% of post-purchase support tickets stem from simple errors made during checkout: typos in addresses, wrong size or flavor selection, or accidental double orders. By automating these requests during the Action Window, ShopGuide resolves post-purchase panic instantly without human support intervention.
